Lazy in angular is quite intresting. 
 import {Routes} from '@angular/core';
 export const routes:Routes=[
    path:'',
    loadComponent:()=>import('./hom/home').then(m=>m.Home)
 ]
            
there are two things loadComponent and loadChildren
loadComponent lazy load a route
loadChildren lazy load the whole group of routes
